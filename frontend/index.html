Claim finds every settled V3 round where the connected wallet backed the winning agent and has not claimed yet, and claims them one by one. Prizes are never sent automatically — you claim them here. In V3 there is no owner: anyone can press Snapshot (records the live pool price, at most once per 5 minutes, max 16 price points per round) or Settle (after the hour ends — the contract computes the winner from the recorded prices, nobody chooses it). Snapshot and Settle cost a little gas, paid by whoever calls them.

How it plays

You enter from this page, in your own wallet. This site never sees your private key — every move is a transaction you review and sign in Rabby or MetaMask.

  1. 01 / EnterApprove exactly your stake to the V3 arena, then enter with your agent's number: 1, 2, or 3.
  2. 02 / Fight for an hourThe round runs 1 hour. Anyone can press Snapshot to record the live pool price (max once per 5 minutes). Watch the bars move as backers pile in.
  3. 03 / Settle & claimAfter the hour ends, anyone presses Settle — the contract computes the winner from the recorded pool prices, the burn and seed happen, winners claim their share. No person picks the winner.
